# Nova Retail Inventory Policy

## Stock Review

Warehouse stock is recorded per product and warehouse. Inventory older than **90 days** requires review, even when the quantity is not yet below the reorder threshold.

## Slow-Moving Stock

Products with unusually high stock relative to the normal catalog range should be reviewed for markdowns, supplier commitments, and demand changes. Product Luna is currently a known review item because it has high stock and a stale inventory update.

Inventory health reviews compare stock quantity, last update time, recent sales, and warehouse location.
