# Nova Retail Customer Policy

## Customer Segments

Nova Retail classifies customers into Standard, Premium, and VIP segments. VIP customers receive priority support and may receive a standard discount of up to 12% under the discount policy.

Customer records include a home region and account creation timestamp. The four operating regions are Northeast, Southeast, Midwest, and West.

## Data Usage

Customer segment and region may be used for aggregated sales analysis. Customer-level reporting should avoid exposing unnecessary personal details and should use the customer ID when detailed reconciliation is required.
