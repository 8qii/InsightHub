# Nova Retail Company Overview

Nova Retail is a fictional e-commerce retailer serving customers across North America. Its operating model combines a direct-to-consumer storefront with regional fulfillment warehouses.

## Business Areas

- Sales: online orders, revenue, and seasonal performance
- Customers: Standard, Premium, and VIP segments across four regions
- Products: consumer electronics, home goods, apparel, and accessories
- Inventory: warehouse stock, replenishment, and aging review
- Payments: payment capture and settlement status for every order
- Discount Management: discount events, approvals, and policy compliance

The Nova Retail reporting calendar uses calendar quarters. The demonstration dataset covers April 1 through September 30, 2025, which provides Q2 and Q3 comparisons for sales analysis.

## Operating Context

Nova Retail has five thousand customers, a catalog of one hundred twenty products, and a high-volume order history. Product Luna is a flagship product under review after a weaker Q3. The business is also tightening discount governance because some orders exceeded the standard VIP limit without manager approval.
