# Nova Retail Product Catalog

The demonstration catalog contains 120 active products across consumer electronics, home goods, apparel, and accessories. Product IDs are stable database identifiers and product names are unique.

## Flagship Product

**Product Luna** is product ID 1, a consumer electronics product priced at $100.00. It remains a strategic product even though Q3 revenue declined compared with Q2. Its inventory position is intentionally high and is included in the inventory review queue.

Prices in this catalog are represented in US dollars. Order amounts are calculated from product price and quantity before any separately recorded discount event.
