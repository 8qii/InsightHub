# Nova Retail Q3 2025 Business Review

## Executive Summary

Nova Retail maintained broad catalog coverage in Q3, but Product Luna revenue declined by approximately **18%** compared with Q2. The decline is material enough to warrant a product and inventory review rather than a routine seasonal explanation.

## Product Luna

Product Luna generated $300,000.00 in Q2 revenue and $246,000.00 in Q3 revenue, an 18% quarter-over-quarter decline. At the same time, the product has unusually high warehouse inventory and an inventory record older than 90 days.

## Discount Governance

The standard VIP discount limit remains 12%. Q3 compliance reporting should identify discount events above 12% that were not approved by a manager. Approved exceptions are tracked separately and should not be counted as policy violations.

## Recommended Actions

1. Review Product Luna demand, pricing, and inventory commitments.
2. Investigate unapproved discount exceptions by region.
3. Reconcile payment status before using operational order counts in financial reporting.
