# Nova Retail Sales Policy

## Revenue Reporting

Revenue is reported from the `orders.amount` field. Quarterly revenue is grouped by `order_date` using calendar quarters. Q2 covers April through June and Q3 covers July through September.

Orders are associated with one customer and one product. Payment records confirm the settlement state but do not change the recorded order amount.

## Discounted Sales

Discount events are audited separately from order revenue. A discount event applies to an order and records the requested percentage, whether it was approved, and the event creation time.

Sales managers should review revenue trends by product, customer segment, and region before changing targets or inventory commitments.
